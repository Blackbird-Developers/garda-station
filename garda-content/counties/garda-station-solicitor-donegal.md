If you have been arrested in Donegal, or asked to attend a Garda station to be interviewed, you can speak to a solicitor before you answer anything. Ring 087 122 3080. We will tell you what we can do, and we will tell you if we cannot get there in time.

## Where you are likely to be held

Letterkenny is the largest town in the county and its station carries the biggest share of custody work. Ballybofey and Stranorlar cover the Finn valley, with Lifford on the border at Strabane.

On Inishowen, arrests are commonly processed at Buncrana and Carndonagh, with Moville and Burnfoot covering the east of the peninsula. In the south, Donegal town, Ballyshannon and Bundoran take in most of the work, and Killybegs covers the fishing port. In the west, Milford, Glenties, Dungloe and Falcarragh serve a large and thinly populated area, much of it Gaeltacht.

> **If the Gardai are waiting**
> Give your name, address and date of birth and no more. Say clearly that you want a solicitor before any interview and ask the member in charge to ring us. Ring 087 122 3080.

## Where Donegal cases are heard

District Court sittings are held around the county, including at Letterkenny, Donegal Town, Ballyshannon, Buncrana, Carndonagh, Glenties, Dungloe, Falcarragh and Milford. The Circuit Criminal Court sits at Letterkenny and at Donegal Town.

The spread of venues here is wider than in almost any other county, so check the courthouse named on your charge sheet rather than assuming.

## Donegal is the furthest county from us, and we will say so

Ferrys Solicitors LLP has five offices and all five are in Dublin. There is no Donegal office and we are not going to invent one.

Letterkenny is roughly 230 kilometres from Dublin, but the number understates it. There is no motorway for most of the route, and the usual road runs north through Monaghan and across the border through Tyrone before coming back into the State. A realistic drive is three to three and a half hours. Inishowen, Glenties or Falcarragh add a good deal more on top.

So we do not open with a promise to be there. We open with what actually helps at two in the morning. Six criminal defence solicitors work a rota, so a solicitor can normally speak to you by telephone very quickly, and that call can happen before any interview begins.

For serious matters we do travel to Donegal. When you ring, we will tell you honestly how long it would take, and if that is not fast enough we will help you find a solicitor closer to the station you are in.

## What to do in the meantime

1. Ask the member in charge to record your request for a solicitor.
2. Ask for a private telephone consultation without waiting for anyone to travel.
3. Say nothing about the allegation to anyone in the station.
4. Do not sign a statement, memo of interview or caution form beforehand.
5. Note the station, the time of arrest and any Garda who spoke to you.
