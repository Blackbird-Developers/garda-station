If you are being held in a Garda station anywhere in Kildare, you can ask for a solicitor before you are interviewed, and you should. Ring 087 122 3080 and we will tell you straight away what we can do.

## Stations across the county

Naas is the county town and its station carries a large share of the custody work. Newbridge and Kildare town are busy in their own right, and Athy covers the south of the county.

Along the Dublin border the commuter towns each have their own station: Leixlip, Celbridge, Maynooth, Kilcock and Clane. Further south and west, arrests may be processed at Monasterevin, Rathangan, Kill, Carbury or Castledermot.

Kildare also carries two of the busiest road corridors in the country, so a significant amount of station work here begins with a road traffic stop on the M7 or the M4.

> **If the Gardai are waiting**
> Give only your name, address and date of birth. Say clearly that you want a solicitor before any interview and ask the member in charge to phone us. Then wait. Ring 087 122 3080.

## Where Kildare cases are heard

District Court business for the county is heard mainly at Naas Courthouse, with sittings also held in Athy. Naas is the Circuit Court venue for Kildare, so cases returned for trial are generally listed there.

If your charge sheet gives a court, a date and a time, keep it somewhere safe and photograph it. Missing a first appearance creates a separate problem on top of the one you already have.

## Steps to take now

1. Ask the member in charge to record your request for a solicitor.
2. Ask for telephone advice immediately rather than waiting for an arrival.
3. Do not answer questions about the allegation until you have taken that advice.
4. Do not sign a statement or memo of interview.
5. If you are the family member ringing, have the station name and the time of arrest ready.

Often yes. The M7 is a straight run and Kildare is one of the counties we reach most easily. Ring and we will give you an honest time.

Ask the member in charge which station you are in and ask them to ring us with that detail. Where you are matters for how fast anyone can get to you.

### Is advice at the station free?

Advice for a person in custody is generally covered under the State scheme. Ask us on the phone and we will explain what applies in your case.
