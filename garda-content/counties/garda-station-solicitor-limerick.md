If you are being held in a Garda station in Limerick, or you have been asked to attend one for interview, you can ask for a solicitor before anyone questions you. Ring 087 122 3080 at any hour and we will tell you what we can do.

## Stations in the city

Henry Street is the principal city station and takes a large share of custody work for Limerick city. Roxboro Road covers the southside and the eastern approaches, and Mayorstone Park covers the northside.

Limerick is the third largest city in the State. Its stations deal with the full range of criminal work, from public order and road traffic matters through to serious investigations where interviews are long and detention may be extended. If you are in one of them, the moment to ask for advice is before the first interview, not after it.

## Stations across the county

West Limerick work is generally processed at Newcastle West, Abbeyfeale, Rathkeale and Askeaton. In the south and east of the county, arrests may be dealt with at Bruff, Kilmallock, Cappamore, Croom, Adare or Patrickswell.

> **If the Gardai are waiting**
> Give your name, address and date of birth. Then say clearly that you want a solicitor before any interview and ask the member in charge to phone us. Do not fill the silence. Ring 087 122 3080.

## Where Limerick cases are heard

District Court and Circuit Criminal Court business for Limerick is heard at Limerick Courthouse on Mulgrave Street. District Court sittings are also held in the county, including at Newcastle West and Kilmallock.

If you have been charged and released, your charge sheet will name a court, a date and a time. Photograph it and keep it safe. Failing to turn up creates a fresh problem on top of the one you already have.

## What our cover in Limerick means in practice

Ferrys Solicitors LLP has five offices and every one is in Dublin. We have no Limerick office and we are not going to imply one.

What we can tell you honestly is that Limerick is one of the more reachable cities from Dublin. The M7 runs almost the whole way, roughly 200 kilometres, which is around two hours in normal conditions. Newcastle West and Abbeyfeale add a good deal more on top of that, because the road west of the city is slower.

Six criminal defence solicitors work a rota here, so there is generally someone free to take your call and give advice by phone within minutes. Whether one of them can be standing in the station in time is a separate question, and we will answer it plainly on the call rather than leave you guessing.

## Steps to take now

1. Ask the member in charge to record your request for a solicitor.
2. Ask for telephone advice immediately; do not wait for someone to travel.
3. Do not discuss the allegation with Gardai or with anyone else in the station.
4. Do not sign anything until you have had advice.
5. If you are ringing for a family member, have the station name and time of arrest ready.

## Common questions

### How quickly can a solicitor speak to me at Henry Street?

Usually very quickly by telephone. A call from a solicitor can be arranged through the member in charge long before anyone could arrive in person.

### Do you have an office in Limerick?

No. All five offices are in Dublin. We act for clients nationwide and travel for serious matters.

### Can my partner ring on my behalf?

Yes. If you cannot get to a phone, someone else can ring 087 122 3080 for you. We will need the station and, if known, what you have been arrested for.

### Is advice at the station free?

Advice for a person in custody is generally covered under the State scheme. Ask us on the phone and we will explain what applies in your situation.
