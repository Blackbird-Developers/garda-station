If you have been arrested in Clare, or a Garda has asked you to attend a station for interview, you can take legal advice before you answer anything. Ring 087 122 3080 and we will give you a straight answer about what we can do tonight.

## Where you are likely to be held

Ennis is the county town and its station handles the largest volume of custody work in Clare. Shannon covers the town, the industrial estate and the airport. Sixmilebridge and Newmarket-on-Fergus sit along the same corridor.

In west Clare, arrests are commonly processed at Kilrush, Kilkee, Milltown Malbay and Ennistymon. In the east, along the Shannon and Lough Derg, Killaloe and Scariff cover a large and sparsely populated area.

## Arrests connected with Shannon Airport

Clare is unusual in that a busy international airport sits inside the county. Matters that begin at an airport can involve documents, baggage, travel dates and onward flights, and they often need decisions made quickly. If your situation involves the airport, say so when you ring, because it changes what needs attention first.

> **If the Gardai are waiting**
> Give your name, address and date of birth, then say nothing further about the allegation. Ask for a solicitor before any interview and ask the member in charge to ring us. Ring 087 122 3080.

## Where Clare cases are heard

District Court business for the county is heard principally at Ennis Courthouse, with sittings also held at Kilrush, Ennistymon and Killaloe. The Circuit Criminal Court sits in Ennis for matters returned for trial.

## What attendance from Dublin actually looks like

Ferrys Solicitors LLP has five offices and all five are in Dublin. There is no Clare office and we will not suggest there is.

Ennis is around 250 kilometres from us. The M7 and M18 carry you most of the way, so it is roughly two and a half hours in reasonable conditions, and Shannon is a little closer. West Clare is slower going: Kilrush, Kilkee and the Burren villages add a further hour or more, and the roads there are not built for speed.

We field six criminal defence solicitors on a rota, so getting a solicitor on the phone at three in the morning does not depend on one person happening to be awake. Telephone advice can be arranged quickly. Whether one of us can be physically present before you are interviewed is a different question, and we will answer it honestly on the call rather than promise and disappoint.

## What to do while you wait

1. Ask the member in charge to record that you have requested a solicitor.
2. Ask for telephone advice immediately.
3. Say nothing about the allegation to anyone in the station.
4. Do not sign a statement or memo of interview before you have taken advice.
5. Note the station, the time of arrest and any Garda who dealt with you.
