If you have been arrested in Louth, or asked to attend a Garda station for interview, you can speak to a solicitor before you answer any questions. Ring 087 122 3080 and we will tell you honestly what we can do and how quickly.

## Where you are likely to be held

The busiest stations in the county are Drogheda and Dundalk, which cover the two largest towns in Louth and a good deal of the surrounding area. Outside those, arrests are commonly processed at Ardee, Dunleer and Carlingford.

Drogheda is one of the largest towns in the country by population, and its station handles a heavy volume of custody work. Dundalk sits close to the border, and matters arising there can involve cross border elements that are worth raising with a solicitor early.

> **If the Gardai are waiting**
> Give your name, address and date of birth. Say clearly that you want a solicitor before any interview, and ask the member in charge to ring us. Do not discuss the allegation with anyone until we have spoken. Ring 087 122 3080.

## Where Louth cases are heard

District Court business for the county is heard principally at Dundalk Courthouse on Crowe Street and at Drogheda Courthouse on Fair Street, with sittings also held in Ardee. More serious matters returned for trial are dealt with by the Circuit Criminal Court, which sits in Dundalk.

When you ring, we will tell you plainly whether we can be there in time. If we cannot, we will say so and help you find a solicitor who can. That is a better answer than one that sounds impressive at midnight and leaves you sitting in a cell.

## What to do while you wait

1. Ask the member in charge to record that you have requested a solicitor.
2. Say nothing about the allegation, to Gardai or to anyone else in the station.
3. Do not sign a statement, a memo of interview or a caution form until you have had advice.
4. Ask a family member to ring 087 122 3080 on your behalf if you cannot.
5. Note the station, the time of arrest and the name of any Garda who spoke to you.

## Common questions

### Can you get to Drogheda or Dundalk tonight?

Often, yes. The M1 makes Louth one of the more reachable counties from Dublin. Ring and we will give you a realistic time rather than a promise.

### Can I get advice by phone before you arrive?

Yes. A solicitor can speak to you by telephone in the station, and that call can happen well before anyone travels. Ask the member in charge to arrange it.

### Do you have an office in Louth?

No. Our five offices are all in Dublin. We act for clients across the country and travel for serious matters, and we would rather tell you that straight.

### I am charged and due in Dundalk District Court. Will you appear?

We appear in District Courts around the country. Ring us with your court date and the charge sheet and we will tell you honestly whether we can cover it.

### Does the right to silence protect me completely?

You have a right to silence, but in certain limited circumstances a court may be permitted to draw inferences from a failure to answer. That is exactly why the decision should be made with legal advice.
