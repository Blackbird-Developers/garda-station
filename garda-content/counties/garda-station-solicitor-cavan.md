If you are being held in a Garda station in Cavan, or you have been asked to attend one for interview, you can speak to a solicitor before you are questioned. Ring 087 122 3080 and we will tell you honestly what we can do and how soon.

## Where you are likely to be held

Cavan town station handles the largest volume of custody work in the county. Bailieborough and Cootehill cover the east, towards the Monaghan and Meath borders, and Virginia and Kingscourt cover the south east.

In the west of the county, Belturbet and Ballyconnell serve the area towards Fermanagh, and Ballyjamesduff and Arva cover the centre and south.

Cavan is a border county of small towns and a great many lakes, with no large urban centre. Journeys within the county take longer than the map suggests, because the road network winds around water.

> **If the Gardai are waiting**
> Give your name, address and date of birth, then stop talking about the allegation. Ask for a solicitor before any interview and ask the member in charge to ring us. Ring 087 122 3080.

## Where Cavan cases are heard

District Court business for the county is heard principally at Cavan Courthouse, with sittings also held at Bailieborough. The Circuit Criminal Court sits in Cavan for matters returned for trial.

If you have been charged and released, photograph the charge sheet. It names the venue, the date and the time you must attend.

The good news is the distance. Cavan town is around 120 kilometres from Dublin, and the M3 carries you most of the way north before the N3 takes over past Kells. ## Steps to take now

1. Ask the member in charge to record your request for a solicitor.
2. Ask for a private telephone consultation straight away.
3. Do not discuss the allegation with Gardai or anyone else in the station.
4. Do not sign a statement, memo or caution form before taking advice.
5. If you are ringing for a family member, have the station name and the time of arrest ready.
