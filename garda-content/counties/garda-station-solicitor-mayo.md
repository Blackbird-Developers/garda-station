If you have been arrested in Mayo, or a Garda has asked you to attend a station for interview, you can speak to a solicitor before you answer any questions. Ring 087 122 3080 and we will give you an honest answer about what we can do.

## Where you are likely to be held

Castlebar is the county town and its station carries a large share of custody work. Ballina in the north and Westport in the west are the other principal stations.

Around the county, arrests are also processed at Claremorris, Swinford, Ballyhaunis, Charlestown, Kiltimagh, Foxford, Ballinrobe and Newport. Out on the Atlantic edge, Belmullet covers the Erris peninsula and Achill covers the island and its approaches.

Mayo is one of the largest counties by area and much of it is coastline, bog and mountain. The distance between two stations in the same county can be well over an hour.

> **If the Gardai are waiting**
> Give your name, address and date of birth, then stop. Say that you want a solicitor before any interview and ask the member in charge to ring us on your behalf. Ring 087 122 3080.

## Where Mayo cases are heard

District Court business is heard at Castlebar Courthouse and at venues around the county including Ballina, Westport, Swinford and Belmullet. The Circuit Criminal Court sits in Castlebar for matters returned for trial.

Photograph your charge sheet if you have been charged and released. In a county with this many sitting venues, checking the correct courthouse is not a formality.

## What the journey from Dublin means

Ferrys Solicitors LLP has five offices and every one of them is in Dublin. We have no Mayo office and we are not going to imply one.

Castlebar is around 250 kilometres from us. The M4 becomes the N4 and then the N5, and only part of that is dual carriageway, so a realistic drive is about three hours. Ballina is similar. Belmullet and Achill are a further hour and a half beyond Castlebar, on roads that punish any attempt to hurry.

Because six criminal defence solicitors work a rota rather than one person carrying one phone, you can normally get a solicitor on the line quickly at any hour of the night. Telephone advice before an interview is genuine advice and it does not wait on a car.

On attendance, we will be straight with you. For a serious matter we will travel to Mayo. If your detention is likely to be over before anyone could arrive, we will say so rather than leave you hoping, and we will help you find a solicitor closer to you.

## What to do now

1. Ask the member in charge to record that you have requested a solicitor.
2. Ask for a private telephone consultation immediately.
3. Say nothing about the allegation to anyone in the station.
4. Do not sign a statement or memo of interview before taking advice.
5. Note the station, the time of arrest and the name of any Garda who spoke to you.

## Common questions

### Do you have an office in Mayo?

No. All five offices are in Dublin. We act for clients nationwide and travel for serious matters, and we would rather be clear about that.

### I am held in Belmullet or Achill. What is realistic?

Telephone advice is realistic and immediate. Attendance in person from Dublin is a long journey and we will tell you honestly how long it would take.

### Can my family ring for me?

Yes. If you cannot get to a phone, someone else can ring 087 122 3080. They should have the station name and the time of arrest.

### Does the right to silence protect me completely?

You have a right to silence, but in certain limited circumstances a court may be permitted to draw inferences from a failure to answer questions. That is why the decision should be taken with legal advice.
