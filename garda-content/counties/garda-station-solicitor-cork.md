If you have been arrested in Cork, or a Garda has asked you to come in for interview, you can speak to a solicitor before you answer any questions. Ring 087 122 3080 and we will give you a straight answer about what we can do and how long it would take.

## Where you are likely to be held

Anglesea Street is the main city centre station and carries much of the custody work for Cork city, with the Bridewell also taking city arrests. On the northside, Gurranabraher and Mayfield are the busy stations; on the southside, Togher, Douglas and Blackrock.

Cork is the second largest city in the State and the largest county by area, so the station you are in matters. Around the county the principal stations include Mallow, Fermoy, Mitchelstown and Charleville in the north, Midleton, Cobh and Youghal in the east, Ballincollig and Carrigaline on the city fringe, and Bandon, Macroom, Clonakilty, Skibbereen and Bantry through West Cork.

> **If the Gardai are waiting**
> Give your name, address and date of birth and nothing else. Say out loud that you want a solicitor before any interview, and ask the member in charge to ring us now. Ring 087 122 3080.

## Where Cork cases are heard

District Court business for the city is heard at the courthouse on Anglesea Street. Matters returned for trial go to the Circuit Criminal Court at the Washington Street courthouse.

Around the county, District Court sittings are held in towns including Mallow, Fermoy, Midleton, Youghal, Bandon, Clonakilty, Skibbereen, Bantry and Macroom. The venue on your charge sheet depends on where the alleged offence happened, not where you live.

## What to do in the meantime

1. Ask the member in charge to record that you have requested a solicitor.
2. Ask for telephone advice straight away rather than waiting on an arrival.
3. Say nothing about the allegation to anyone in the station.
4. Do not sign a statement, memo of interview or caution form before taking advice.
5. Note the station, the time of arrest and any Garda who spoke to you.
