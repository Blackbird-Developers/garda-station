If you are in a Garda station in Kerry, or you have been asked to come in and be interviewed, you can get legal advice first. Ring 087 122 3080 at any hour. We will tell you what we can do and, just as importantly, what we cannot.

## Where you are likely to be held

Tralee is the main station in the county and takes the largest share of custody work. Killarney is busy in its own right, particularly through the tourist season and at weekends.

Beyond those two, arrests are commonly processed at Listowel and Castleisland in the north of the county, at Killorglin and Cahersiveen along the Iveragh peninsula, at Dingle out on the Corca Dhuibhne peninsula, and at Kenmare in the south. Ballybunion covers the north Kerry coast.

Kerry has no city, and the county is spread across long peninsulas with slow roads between them. Where you are held can change the timing of everything that follows.

> **If the Gardai are waiting**
> Give your name, address and date of birth only. Say clearly that you want to speak to a solicitor before any interview, and ask the member in charge to ring us. Ring 087 122 3080.

## Where Kerry cases are heard

District Court business is heard principally at Tralee Courthouse, with sittings also held in Killarney, Listowel, Cahersiveen and Dingle. Matters returned for trial go to the Circuit Criminal Court, which sits in Tralee.

Keep any charge sheet you are given and photograph it. It names the court, the date and the time, and missing that date is a separate difficulty you do not need.

## Distance, honestly

Ferrys Solicitors LLP has five offices and all of them are in Dublin. We have no Kerry office and we are not going to hint at one.

Kerry is one of the furthest counties from us. Tralee is roughly 300 kilometres away, and only part of that journey is motorway; the roads west of Limerick or Mallow are slower. Realistically that is three and a half to four hours. Dingle, Cahersiveen and Kenmare are further again.

So here is how it works. Six criminal defence solicitors share a rota, which means someone can normally speak to you by phone very quickly, at night or at the weekend. Telephone advice before an interview is genuine advice and it costs you no travelling time at all.

Attendance in person is a different question and we will answer it straight on the call. For a serious matter we will travel to Kerry. If the detention is likely to be over before a car could get there, we will tell you that rather than let you sit waiting, and we will help you find a solicitor closer to you.

## What to do right now

1. Ask the member in charge to record your request for a solicitor.
2. Ask for a telephone consultation immediately.
3. Say nothing about the allegation until you have had that call.
4. Do not sign a statement, memo or caution form beforehand.
5. Note the station, the time of arrest and any Garda who spoke to you.

### I am on holiday in Killarney and have been arrested. What now?

Ring us. Being away from home complicates practical things like bail addresses and return dates, and those are worth sorting out early.

### Will you appear in Tralee District Court?

We appear in District Courts around the country. Ring with the date, venue and charge and we will tell you honestly whether we can cover it.
