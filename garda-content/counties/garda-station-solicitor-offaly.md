If you have been arrested in Offaly, or a Garda has asked you to come in for interview, you can take legal advice before you answer anything. Ring 087 122 3080 and we will tell you what we can realistically do.

## Where arrests are processed

Tullamore is the county town and its station handles the bulk of custody work in Offaly. Birr, in the south of the county near the Tipperary border, is the other station of real size.

Edenderry sits in the north east, closest to Kildare and the Dublin road, and is busy for its size. Elsewhere in the county arrests may be dealt with at Clara, Ferbane, Banagher on the Shannon, or Kilcormac.

Offaly is a long county from north east to south west, and the station you are brought to can be well away from where you were stopped. If a family member is trying to find you, ask them to establish the station name first.

> **If the Gardai are waiting**
> Give your name, address and date of birth. Say clearly that you want a solicitor before any interview and ask the member in charge to phone us. Then stop talking about the allegation. Ring 087 122 3080.

## Where Offaly cases are heard

District Court sittings for the county are held at Tullamore Courthouse and at Birr Courthouse. Tullamore is also the Circuit Court venue for Offaly, so a case returned for trial is generally listed there.

Check the venue on your charge sheet. Tullamore and Birr are a good distance apart and the wrong courthouse on the day causes real trouble.

## What to do now

1. Ask the member in charge to record your request for a solicitor.
2. Ask for telephone advice immediately.
3. Do not discuss the allegation with anyone, including others in custody.
4. Do not sign anything until you have taken advice.
5. Note the station, the time of arrest and any paperwork you are handed.

## Common questions

### Can I get advice before you arrive?

Yes. Telephone advice in the station is available and is usually the first thing we do. Ask the member in charge to arrange the call.

### Will you come to Birr?

For serious matters we travel. We will tell you the realistic driving time when you ring rather than leave you guessing.

### Do you have an office in Tullamore?

No. All five of our offices are in Dublin. We act for clients nationwide and we would rather be straight about where we are.

### I was asked to attend voluntarily. Is that different?

The interview is still an interview and what you say is still evidence. Take advice before you go in, not after.
