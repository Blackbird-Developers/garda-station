If you are being held in a Garda station in Waterford, or have been asked to attend one for interview, you can speak to a solicitor before you answer any questions. Ring 087 122 3080 and we will tell you plainly what we can do and how quickly.

## Where you are likely to be held

Waterford city station at Ballybricken carries most of the custody work in the county and covers the city and its immediate suburbs. Tramore, a few miles down the coast, gets busy through the summer and at weekends.

In the west of the county, Dungarvan is the principal station, with Lismore, Cappoquin and Tallow covering the Blackwater valley. Along the coast road, Kilmacthomas, Dunmore East and Ardmore serve smaller districts.

Waterford is a city and the oldest one in Ireland, but it is a compact county, which means fewer stations and shorter internal distances than most of Munster.

> **If the Gardai are waiting**
> Give your name, address and date of birth. Then say clearly that you want a solicitor before any interview and ask the member in charge to ring us. Ring 087 122 3080.

## Where Waterford cases are heard

District Court business for the city is heard at Waterford Courthouse, which is also where the Circuit Criminal Court sits for the county. District Court sittings are also held in Dungarvan for the west of the county.

If you have been charged and released, your charge sheet names the court, the date and the time. Photograph it. A missed first appearance becomes a separate problem in its own right.

## What we can honestly offer here

Ferrys Solicitors LLP has five offices and every one is in Dublin. We have no Waterford office and we will not pretend to.

Waterford is, however, one of the more accessible cities from Dublin. The M9 runs most of the way, roughly 160 kilometres, which is around two hours in normal traffic. Dungarvan and the far west of the county add closer to another hour.

Because six criminal defence solicitors share the out of hours rota rather than one phone being answered by one person, there is generally someone available to speak to you promptly, whatever time it is. Telephone advice before an interview can be arranged through the member in charge. For attendance in person we will give you a realistic time on the call, and if we cannot get there before you are questioned we will say so and help you find someone who can.

## Steps to take now

1. Ask the member in charge to record your request for a solicitor.
2. Ask for a private telephone consultation without delay.
3. Do not discuss the allegation with Gardai or anyone else in the station.
4. Do not sign a statement, memo or caution form before taking advice.
5. If you are ringing for someone else, have the station name and time of arrest ready.

## Common questions

### Can you get to Waterford at night?

Often, for a serious matter. The M9 is a straight run. Ring and we will give you a time rather than a promise.

### Do you have an office in Waterford?

No. Our five offices are all in Dublin. We act for clients across the country and travel for serious matters.

### My son has been arrested in Tramore and I cannot get information. What can I do?

Ring 087 122 3080 with the station name and the time of arrest. A solicitor can make contact with the station on his behalf and find out where things stand.

### Is advice at the station free?

Advice for a person in custody is generally covered under the State scheme. Ask us on the call and we will explain what applies to you.
