If you have been arrested in Leitrim, or asked to come into a Garda station for interview, you can speak to a solicitor before you are questioned. Ring 087 122 3080 and we will tell you honestly what we can do and when.

## Where you are likely to be held

Carrick-on-Shannon is the county town and its station carries the largest share of custody work in Leitrim. Ballinamore, Mohill and Drumshanbo cover much of the south of the county.

North Leitrim is served from Manorhamilton, with Dromahair and Kinlough covering the areas towards Lough Gill and the coast.

Leitrim has the smallest population of any county in Ireland, and it is effectively cut in two by Lough Allen and the high ground around it. Getting from the south of the county to the north is not a short trip, and that is worth understanding when you are told which station you are being brought to.

> **If the Gardai are waiting**
> Give your name, address and date of birth only. Say clearly that you want a solicitor before any interview and ask the member in charge to ring us. Ring 087 122 3080.

## Where Leitrim cases are heard

District Court business for the county is heard principally at Carrick-on-Shannon Courthouse, with sittings also held at Manorhamilton for north Leitrim. The Circuit Criminal Court sits at Carrick-on-Shannon.

If you have been charged and released, photograph the charge sheet. It names the court, the date and the time.

## Being honest about getting to you

Ferrys Solicitors LLP has five offices and every one is in Dublin. We have no Leitrim office and we are not going to suggest we do.

Carrick-on-Shannon is roughly 160 kilometres from Dublin. The M4 gives way to the N4 well before you reach Longford, so the last stretch is single carriageway. In practice it is about two hours, sometimes a little more. Manorhamilton and north Leitrim are a further forty minutes or so beyond that, and closer to Sligo than to anywhere else.

We field six criminal defence solicitors on a rota, so a call at any hour is generally answered by a solicitor who is actually available, not by a voicemail. Telephone advice before an interview can be arranged quickly through the member in charge.

Attendance in person is a different calculation and we will do it out loud with you on the call. For a serious matter we will travel. If we cannot get there before you are interviewed, we will say so and help you find a solicitor nearer to hand.

## Steps to take now

1. Ask the member in charge to record that you have requested a solicitor.
2. Ask for a private telephone consultation immediately.
3. Say nothing about the allegation to anyone in the station.
4. Do not sign a statement, memo or caution form before taking advice.
5. Note the station, the time of arrest and the name of any Garda who spoke to you.

## Common questions

### Do you have an office in Leitrim?

No. All five offices are in Dublin. We act for clients nationwide and travel for serious matters.

### I am in Manorhamilton, not Carrick. Does that matter?

For your rights, no. For timing, yes. North Leitrim is a longer drive from Dublin, so telephone advice becomes the first practical step.

### My brother has been arrested and I cannot find out anything. Can you help?

Ring us with the station name and the time of arrest. A solicitor can contact the station directly and establish where matters stand.

### Will you appear in Carrick-on-Shannon District Court?

We appear in District Courts around the country. Ring us with the date and the charge sheet and we will tell you honestly whether we can cover it.
