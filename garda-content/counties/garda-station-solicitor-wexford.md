If you have been arrested anywhere in Wexford, or asked to come into a station for interview, you are entitled to speak to a solicitor before you answer questions. Ring 087 122 3080 and we will give you a straight answer about what we can do.

## Stations along a long county

Wexford is a big county and it stretches a long way north to south. Gorey, at the northern end, is closest to Dublin and has grown quickly; its station is busy. Enniscorthy sits in the middle of the county on the Slaney.

Wexford town, in the south east, is the county town and its station handles the largest volume of custody work. New Ross covers the south west towards the Kilkenny and Waterford borders, and Bunclody the north west near the Carlow line. Ferns, Taghmon and Rosslare Harbour take arrests locally.

Rosslare Europort is worth mentioning separately. It is one of the country's main ferry ports, and matters that begin there can involve people who are travelling, sometimes with a boat to catch and sometimes with no local address at all. If that is your position, say so on the phone, because it changes what needs to happen first.

> **If the Gardai are waiting**
> Give your name, address and date of birth. Say clearly that you want a solicitor before any interview and ask the member in charge to phone us. Do not discuss the allegation. Ring 087 122 3080.

## Where Wexford cases are heard

District Court sittings for the county are held at Wexford Courthouse, Gorey Courthouse, Enniscorthy Courthouse and New Ross Courthouse. Which one applies to you generally follows the district the station falls into rather than where you live.

Cases returned for trial go to the Circuit Criminal Court, which sits in Wexford town.

The M11 has changed the north of the county considerably. Gorey is roughly an hour from south Dublin in clear conditions, which makes it one of the more reachable stations on this page. Enniscorthy is longer, and Wexford town and New Ross are a good hour and three quarters or more.

Because six criminal defence solicitors share the out of hours rota, someone will answer and telephone advice can start at once. For a serious matter we will travel. Where the drive means we would arrive too late to be of any use to you, we will tell you that honestly and help you find a solicitor nearer the station instead.

## What to do in the meantime

1. Ask the member in charge to record your request for a solicitor.
2. Ask for telephone advice immediately, before any interview begins.
3. Say nothing about the allegation to Gardai or anyone else in the station.
4. Do not sign a statement or memo of interview until you have taken advice.
5. Note the station, the time of arrest and keep any paperwork you are handed.

## Common questions

### How quickly could you reach Gorey?

Gorey is the quickest part of Wexford for us, thanks to the M11. Ring and we will tell you who is on and how long they would be.

### What if I am due to travel from Rosslare?

Tell us that on the phone. Travel plans, bail and where you can be contacted all interact, and it is better raised at the outset.

### Do you have an office in Wexford?

No. All five offices are in Dublin. We act for clients nationwide and travel for serious matters.

### Can I stay silent in the interview?

You have a right to silence, but in certain limited circumstances a court may be permitted to draw inferences from a failure to answer. That is precisely why you should take advice before deciding.
