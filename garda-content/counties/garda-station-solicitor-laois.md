If you are in custody in Laois, or have been asked to attend a Garda station for interview, you are entitled to speak to a solicitor first. Ring 087 122 3080 and we will tell you honestly what we can do and how long it would take.

## Stations across the county

Portlaoise is the county town and its station carries most of the custody work in Laois. Portarlington in the north east and Mountmellick to the north are the next busiest.

In the south of the county, arrests may be processed at Abbeyleix, Rathdowney, Durrow or Mountrath, and Stradbally covers the area east of Portlaoise. Laois is crossed by the M7 and the M8 diverges within the county, so a significant share of station work here starts with a motorway stop.

> **If the Gardai are waiting**
> Give your name, address and date of birth. Say clearly that you want a solicitor before any interview and ask the member in charge to phone us. Say nothing further about the allegation. Ring 087 122 3080.

## Where Laois cases are heard

District Court business for the county is heard at Portlaoise Courthouse, and cases returned for trial go to the Circuit Criminal Court, which also sits in Portlaoise.

Portlaoise is also where Portlaoise Prison and the Midlands Prison are located. That matters in practice, because if a person is remanded in custody anywhere in the midlands there is a reasonable chance they are held there, and prison visits and consultations are arranged accordingly.

## Steps to take straight away

1. Ask the member in charge to record your request for a solicitor.
2. Ask for telephone advice now, before any interview begins.
3. Do not talk about the allegation to Gardai or to anyone else in the station.
4. Do not sign a statement, memo of interview or any other document until you have taken advice.
5. Note the station, the time of arrest and keep every piece of paper you are given.
