If you have been arrested in Sligo, or asked to attend a Garda station to be interviewed, you can get legal advice before you say anything about the allegation. Ring 087 122 3080 and we will tell you honestly what we can do.

## Where you are likely to be held

Sligo town station on Pearse Road is the main station in the county and handles most custody work. Sligo town is the largest urban centre in the north west, so it draws in matters from a wide area.

Elsewhere in the county, arrests are commonly processed at Ballymote and Tubbercurry in the south, at Collooney in the centre, and at Grange, Enniscrone and Easkey along the coast. Riverstown covers part of the east of the county.

> **If the Gardai are waiting**
> Give your name, address and date of birth and nothing beyond that. Say that you want a solicitor before any interview and ask the member in charge to ring us. Ring 087 122 3080.

## Where Sligo cases are heard

District Court business for the county is heard principally at Sligo Courthouse, with sittings also held at Ballymote and Tubbercurry. The Circuit Criminal Court sits in Sligo for matters returned for trial.

Keep your charge sheet safe and photograph it. It names the venue, the date and the time, and the venue is not always the town nearest you.

## The road from Dublin is the honest issue

Ferrys Solicitors LLP has five offices and all five are in Dublin. There is no Sligo office and we are not going to imply one.

Sligo is around 200 kilometres from Dublin, but unlike the routes south and west, there is no motorway for most of it. The M4 runs out well before Longford and the N4 carries you the rest of the way on single carriageway through Boyle and Collooney. Realistically that is around two and a half to three hours, longer in bad weather or behind traffic.

That is why we lead with what we can do immediately. Six criminal defence solicitors work a rota, so you can normally get a solicitor on the telephone quickly at any hour, and advice by phone before an interview is worth having whether or not anyone travels.

On attendance, we will be direct with you. For a serious matter we will make the drive. If the detention is likely to conclude before we could arrive, we will say so and help you find a solicitor closer to Sligo.

## What to do in the meantime

1. Ask the member in charge to record your request for a solicitor.
2. Ask for telephone advice now rather than waiting on an arrival.
3. Say nothing about the allegation to anyone in the station.
4. Do not sign a statement or memo of interview until you have taken advice.
5. Note the station, the time of arrest and any Garda who spoke to you.

## Common questions

### Do you have an office in Sligo?

No. All five of our offices are in Dublin. We act for clients nationwide and travel for serious matters, and we would rather tell you that straight.

### How fast can a solicitor speak to me?

By telephone, usually quickly. Ask the member in charge to arrange a private call and to note the time you asked.

### I have a date in Sligo District Court. Will you appear?

We appear in District Courts around the country. Ring us with the date, the venue and the charge sheet and we will give you an honest answer.

### Does the right to silence protect me completely?

You have a right to silence, but in certain limited circumstances a court may be permitted to draw inferences from a failure to answer questions. That is exactly why the decision needs advice first.
