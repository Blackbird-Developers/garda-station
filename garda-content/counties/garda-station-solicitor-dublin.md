If you have been arrested in Dublin, or a Garda has phoned and asked you to come into a station for interview, you are entitled to speak to a solicitor before you answer any questions. Dublin is where our criminal defence team is based, and where we are quickest to reach you.

## The stations we attend across the city

Ferrys Solicitors LLP works from five offices in Dublin: Ormond Quay, Rialto, Artane, Ballyfermot and Ballymun. Between them they sit within a short drive of most stations in the Dublin Metropolitan Region.

In the north inner city and north Dublin that includes Store Street, the Bridewell, Mountjoy, Fitzgibbon Street, Cabra, Finglas, Ballymun, Santry, Coolock, Raheny and Clontarf, and further out Blanchardstown, Swords, Malahide, Howth, Balbriggan and the station at Dublin Airport.

South of the Liffey it includes Pearse Street, Kevin Street, Kilmainham, Rathmines, Donnybrook, Irishtown, Terenure, Sundrive Road, Crumlin, Rathfarnham and Tallaght, and in the west Ballyfermot, Clondalkin, Ronanstown and Lucan. In the south east of the county it includes Blackrock, Dun Laoghaire, Dundrum, Cabinteely and Shankill.

If your station is not on that list, ring anyway. These are the busiest, not the limit.

> **If the Gardai are waiting**
> Give your name, your address and your date of birth, say clearly that you want a solicitor before any interview, and ask the member in charge to phone us. Then say nothing else about the allegation until we have spoken. Ring 087 122 3080.

## Where Dublin criminal cases are heard

Most Dublin criminal business is dealt with at the Criminal Courts of Justice on Parkgate Street, Dublin 7. The Dublin Metropolitan District Court sits there, and so do the Circuit Criminal Court, the Central Criminal Court and the Special Criminal Court. Some District Court business for the county is heard outside the city centre, including at Swords, Blanchardstown and Dun Laoghaire.

Tony Collier, our partner in criminal defence, appears in all of those courts, so the solicitor who attends the station is part of the team that will take the case on afterwards.

## What to do while you wait for us

1. Ask the member in charge to record that you have requested a solicitor.
2. Do not discuss the allegation with anyone in the station, including other people in custody.
3. Do not sign anything, including a memo of interview or a statement, until you have taken advice.
4. Ask a family member to ring 087 122 3080 for you if you cannot get to a phone yourself.
5. Write down the time of your arrest and the name of the station as soon as you can.

## Cover at three in the morning

Ours is a rota, not one mobile phone. Six criminal defence solicitors across five Dublin offices means that when you ring at three in the morning from Store Street or Tallaght, somebody is on and somebody can move. The firm has been practising since 1989.

## Common questions

### Can you get to a Dublin station tonight?

Usually yes, and quickly. When you ring we will tell you which of our solicitors is on, roughly how long they will be, and what to do in the meantime.

### Does it cost me anything to have a solicitor at the station?

Advice at the station is generally covered by the State scheme for people in custody. Ask us on the phone and we will explain plainly what applies to you.

### The Gardai asked me to come in voluntarily. Do I still need advice?

Yes. A voluntary attendance is still an interview, and what you say is still evidence. Speak to us before you go in.

### I have been given a court date at Parkgate Street. Can you take it over?

You are entitled to choose your own solicitor at any stage. Ring us with the date and the charge sheet and we will tell you what is involved.
