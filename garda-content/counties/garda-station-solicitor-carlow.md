If you have been arrested in Carlow, or asked to come into a Garda station for interview, you can speak to a solicitor before you answer any questions. Ring 087 122 3080 and we will tell you plainly what we can do.

## A small county with one main station

Carlow is the second smallest county in Ireland and the station network is compact. Carlow town station handles the great majority of custody work in the county, including anything arising in the town itself and in the surrounding area.

Tullow, to the east, is the next busiest. Bagenalstown, also known as Muine Bheag, covers the corridor south along the Barrow, and Borris and Hacketstown serve the more rural south east and the Wicklow border.

Carlow town has a large third level student population, and a share of the work that comes through the town station in term time reflects that: public order allegations, assault allegations arising from nights out, and drug possession. Those are matters where what is said in the first interview can shape everything that follows.

> **If the Gardai are waiting**
> Give your name, address and date of birth. Say clearly that you want a solicitor before any interview and ask the member in charge to phone us. Do not discuss the allegation. Ring 087 122 3080.

## Where Carlow cases are heard

District Court business for the county is heard at Carlow Courthouse in the town, and cases returned for trial go to the Circuit Criminal Court, which also sits in Carlow.

Most people find the courthouse easy enough to reach. What catches people out is the date, so photograph your charge sheet as soon as you are given it.

## What to do while you wait

1. Ask the member in charge to record your request for a solicitor.
2. Ask for telephone advice now rather than waiting for someone to arrive.
3. Say nothing about the allegation to anyone in the station.
4. Do not sign a statement or memo of interview until you have taken advice.
5. Note the station, the time of arrest and any paperwork you are given.

## Common questions

### Can you be in Carlow tonight?

Possibly, depending on the hour and who is on the rota. Ring and you will get a realistic time rather than a reassurance.

### I am a student and worried about my course. Should I say that?

Say it to us, not to the Gardai. Consequences for study, employment and vetting are things a solicitor needs to know about early, because they can affect how a case is approached.

### Do you have an office in Carlow?

No. All five offices are in Dublin. We act for clients nationwide and travel for serious matters.

### My son has been arrested and he is seventeen. What happens?

A young person under eighteen has additional protections, including in relation to a parent or guardian being present. Ring us straight away and we will talk you through it.
