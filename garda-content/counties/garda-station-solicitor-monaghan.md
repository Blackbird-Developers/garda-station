If you have been arrested in Monaghan, or asked to come into a Garda station for interview, you are entitled to legal advice before any questioning. Ring 087 122 3080 and we will tell you plainly what we can do.

## Where you are likely to be held

Monaghan town station covers the north of the county and the approaches to the Armagh and Tyrone borders. Carrickmacross serves the south, and Castleblayney sits between the two on the main Dublin road.

Clones covers the west of the county, where the border wraps around on three sides, and Ballybay, Emyvale and Scotstown take in the remaining rural districts.

Monaghan is a border county of drumlins and small towns, with no city and no single large centre. Roads here loop and climb, and a journey that looks short on a map rarely is.

> **If the Gardai are waiting**
> Give your name, address and date of birth. Then say that you want a solicitor before any interview and ask the member in charge to phone us. Ring 087 122 3080.

## Where Monaghan cases are heard

District Court business is heard principally at Monaghan Courthouse, with sittings also held at Carrickmacross and Castleblayney. The Circuit Criminal Court sits in Monaghan for matters returned for trial.

Keep the charge sheet and photograph it. It tells you which courthouse, which day and what time, and turning up at the wrong one causes trouble you do not need.

## What to do while you wait

1. Ask the member in charge to record that you have requested a solicitor.
2. Ask for a private telephone consultation without delay.
3. Say nothing about the allegation to anyone in the station.
4. Do not sign a statement, memo of interview or caution form beforehand.
5. Note the station, the time of arrest and the name of any Garda who spoke to you.

## Common questions

### Do you have an office in Monaghan?

No. All five offices are in Dublin. We act for clients nationwide and travel for serious matters, and we prefer to say that up front.

### I live in Northern Ireland but was arrested in Monaghan. What now?

Ask for a solicitor and say nothing about the allegation until you have advice. Living outside the State affects practical matters such as bail and return dates, so raise it on the call.

### Can someone else ring for me?

Yes. A family member can ring 087 122 3080 on your behalf. They should have the station name and, if known, the time of arrest.

### Does the right to silence protect me completely?

You have a right to silence, but in certain limited circumstances a court may be permitted to draw inferences from a failure to answer questions. That is exactly why the decision should be made with legal advice first.
