If you have been arrested in Roscommon, or asked to attend a Garda station for interview, you can take legal advice before you answer any questions. Ring 087 122 3080 and we will tell you honestly what we can do.

## Where you are likely to be held

Roscommon town station covers the county town and the surrounding area. Castlerea and Ballaghaderreen serve the west of the county, and Boyle covers the north, close to the Sligo and Leitrim borders.

Other stations taking arrests around the county include Strokestown, Elphin and Athleague. Roscommon is a long, thin county with no large urban centre, so the station you are brought to can be a considerable distance from your home.

Castlerea is also the location of a prison, which means the town features in a good deal of criminal justice traffic that has nothing to do with local arrests. If your call relates to someone in custody there rather than in a Garda station, say so at the start, because the position is different.

> **If the Gardai are waiting**
> Give your name, address and date of birth. Then say that you want a solicitor before any interview and ask the member in charge to phone us. Ring 087 122 3080.

## Where Roscommon cases are heard

District Court business is heard principally at Roscommon Courthouse, with sittings also held at Boyle and Castlerea. The Circuit Criminal Court sits in Roscommon town for matters returned for trial.

Photograph any charge sheet you are given. It names the court, the date and the time, and the venue may not be the town closest to you.

For attendance in person, we will give you a realistic time on the call. For a serious matter we will travel. If we cannot be there before you are interviewed, we will tell you that plainly and help you find a solicitor who can.

## What to do while you wait

1. Ask the member in charge to record your request for a solicitor.
2. Ask for telephone advice straight away.
3. Do not discuss the allegation with anyone in the station.
4. Do not sign a statement, memo or caution form until you have taken advice.
5. Note the station, the time of arrest and any Garda who spoke to you.

### Do you have an office in Roscommon?

No. Our five offices are all in Dublin. We act for clients nationwide and travel for serious matters.

### Can advice be given over the phone in the station?

Yes. The member in charge can arrange a private telephone consultation with a solicitor, and that is usually the quickest form of help available to you.

### Is advice at the station free?

Advice for a person in custody is generally covered under the State scheme. Ask us on the call and we will explain what applies in your case.

We appear in District Courts around the country. Ring us with the date, venue and charge sheet and we will give you an honest answer.
