If you have been arrested in Wicklow, or a Garda has asked you to attend a station for interview, you can speak to a solicitor first. Ring 087 122 3080 and we will tell you what is possible tonight.

## The county splits in two

Wicklow is not one place for these purposes. The north of the county runs into Dublin: Bray station is the busiest in Wicklow and sits minutes from the Dublin border, and Greystones, Enniskerry and Newtownmountkennedy serve the same commuter corridor.

The rest of the county is a longer journey. Wicklow town, Arklow and Rathdrum cover the east coast south of Greystones. Blessington and Dunlavin serve the west, on the Kildare side of the mountains. Baltinglass, Tinahely, Carnew, Aughrim and Ashford cover the inland south, and travel there from Dublin is slower than the map suggests because the mountain roads are not motorway.

> **If the Gardai are waiting**
> Give your name, address and date of birth, then say you want a solicitor before any interview and ask the member in charge to ring us. Say nothing else about the allegation. Ring 087 122 3080.

## Where Wicklow cases are heard

District Court sittings for the county are held at Bray Courthouse, Wicklow Courthouse, Arklow Courthouse and Baltinglass Courthouse. Which one your case is listed in generally follows the district the station falls into.

More serious matters returned for trial go to the Circuit Criminal Court, which sits in Wicklow town and in Bray. Tony Collier, our partner in criminal defence, appears in District, Circuit Criminal, Central Criminal and Special Criminal Courts.

## Attendance: the honest position

Ferrys Solicitors LLP has five offices and all five are in Dublin. There is no Wicklow office and we will not claim one.

For north Wicklow that matters very little. Bray and Greystones are reachable from our south Dublin side quickly, and the M11 and the M50 make it a straightforward run at most hours. For Arklow, Baltinglass or Tinahely the journey is real and we will say so rather than let you sit waiting.

Because six criminal defence solicitors share the out of hours rota, there is generally someone to answer and someone free to move. When you ring, you will get a realistic time. If we cannot get there soon enough, we will help you find a solicitor who can.

## What to do in the meantime

1. Ask the member in charge to record that you have requested a solicitor.
2. Ask for telephone advice straight away; it does not require anyone to travel.
3. Do not discuss the allegation with Gardai or with anyone else in the station.
4. Do not sign a statement or a memo of interview until you have taken advice.
5. Note the station, the time of arrest and the names of any Gardai involved.

## Common questions

### How fast can you get to Bray?

Bray is one of the quickest non Dublin stations for us to reach. Ring and we will tell you who is on and how long they will be.

### What about Arklow or Baltinglass?

Those are a longer drive and we will be straight with you about the time. If speed matters more than anything else, we will say so and help you find someone nearby.

### Can I refuse to be interviewed until my solicitor arrives?

You can ask for a solicitor and ask that the interview wait. Whether it does is not entirely in your hands, which is why telephone advice at once is so useful.

### Does a caution or charge in Wicklow show up on Garda vetting?

Vetting outcomes depend on what happens with the case, not simply on being arrested. Raise it with us early, because it often shapes how a case is approached.
