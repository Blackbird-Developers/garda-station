If you have been arrested in Meath, or a Garda has asked you to come in for interview, you are entitled to legal advice before you answer anything. Ring 087 122 3080 and we will tell you what we can realistically do tonight.

## The main stations in the county

Navan is the county town and its station handles the largest share of custody work in Meath. Ashbourne and Dunboyne serve the fast growing commuter belt on the Dublin side of the county, and both are busy.

Trim and Kells cover the west and north west, Athboy and Enfield the areas towards Westmeath and Kildare, and Laytown covers the east Meath coastal strip around Bettystown and Julianstown. Duleek, Slane and Ratoath also take arrests locally.

> **If the Gardai are waiting**
> Give your name, address and date of birth, then say you want a solicitor before any interview and ask the member in charge to phone us. Say nothing about the allegation in the meantime. Ring 087 122 3080.

## Where Meath criminal matters are heard

District Court sittings for the county are held at Navan Courthouse and at Trim Courthouse, with further sittings in Kells and Dunshaughlin. Trim is the Circuit Court venue for Meath, so if a case is returned for trial it is generally there that it goes.

Meath is a large county with several courthouses, and the venue you are given often follows the district the station sits in rather than where you live. Check the date and the venue on your charge sheet carefully.

## What to do first

1. Ask the member in charge to note your request for a solicitor.
2. Ask for a phone consultation now rather than waiting for someone to arrive.
3. Do not sign anything, including a memo of interview, until you have had advice.
4. Say nothing about the allegation to anyone in the station.
5. Have a family member ring 087 122 3080 for you if you cannot get to a phone.
