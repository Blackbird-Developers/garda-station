If you have been arrested in Kilkenny, or a Garda has asked you to attend a station for interview, you can take legal advice before you answer anything. Ring 087 122 3080 and we will tell you honestly what is possible.

## Stations in the city and the county

Kilkenny city station handles the largest share of custody work in the county. It covers the city centre, which is compact, heavily visited and busy at weekends, so a good deal of public order and assault work comes through it.

Around the county, arrests may be processed at Castlecomer in the north, Callan to the west, Thomastown and Graiguenamanagh in the south east along the Nore and the Barrow, and Urlingford, Freshford, Ballyragget or Piltown depending on where the matter arose.

Kilkenny draws very large crowds for festivals and for hurling weekends. If you have been arrested during one of those, the station is likely to be under pressure and processing may be slower than usual. That is frustrating, but it is not a reason to start answering questions to speed things up.

> **If the Gardai are waiting**
> Give your name, address and date of birth. Say clearly that you want a solicitor before any interview and ask the member in charge to phone us. Then say nothing about the allegation. Ring 087 122 3080.

## Where Kilkenny cases are heard

District Court business for the county is heard at Kilkenny Courthouse on Parliament Street in the city. The Circuit Criminal Court also sits in Kilkenny, so cases returned for trial are generally listed there.

Keep the charge sheet safe and photograph it. The date and the courtroom are on it, and a missed first appearance creates a fresh problem on top of the original one.

## What to do first

1. Ask the member in charge to record your request for a solicitor.
2. Ask for a telephone consultation before any interview starts.
3. Do not discuss the allegation with anyone in the station.
4. Do not sign a statement or memo of interview until you have had advice.
5. Note the station name and the time of arrest.
