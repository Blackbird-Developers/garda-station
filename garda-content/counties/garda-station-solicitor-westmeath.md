If you are in custody in Westmeath, or have been asked to come into a station for interview, you are entitled to legal advice before you answer questions. Ring 087 122 3080 and we will tell you honestly what we can do.

## The two centres, and everything between

Westmeath has two towns of real size and they sit at opposite ends of the county. Mullingar is the county town, and its station handles the largest volume of custody work in the east. Athlone sits on the Shannon at the western edge and is the other major station, serving a large catchment on both sides of the river.

Between and around them, arrests may be processed at Moate, Kilbeggan, Kinnegad, Rochfortbridge, Tyrrellspass, Delvin, Castlepollard or Ballynacargy. Kinnegad in particular sits at the junction of the main routes west, so road traffic matters there are common.

> **If the Gardai are waiting**
> Give your name, address and date of birth. Say clearly that you want a solicitor before any interview and ask the member in charge to phone us. Say nothing about the allegation. Ring 087 122 3080.

## Where Westmeath cases are heard

District Court business for the county is heard at Mullingar Courthouse and at Athlone Courthouse. Mullingar is the Circuit Court venue for Westmeath, so cases returned for trial are generally listed there.

If you are given a charge sheet, check the courthouse named on it. Athlone and Mullingar are the better part of an hour apart, and turning up at the wrong one counts as not turning up at all.

## What to do first

1. Ask the member in charge to record your request for a solicitor.
2. Ask for telephone advice now. That can happen in minutes, not hours.
3. Say nothing about the allegation to anyone in the station.
4. Do not sign a statement or memo of interview until you have taken advice.
5. Note the station name, the time of arrest and any Garda you dealt with.
