Arrests do not check the map first. If you have been arrested anywhere in Ireland, or asked to attend a Garda station for interview, you can speak to a criminal defence solicitor before you answer any questions. Ring 087 122 3080, at any hour, and you will get advice straight away and a straight answer about attendance.

## Where we are, said plainly

Ferrys Solicitors LLP has five offices, and all five are in Dublin. We do not have a branch in your county, and we will not pretend otherwise.

What we do have is a rota of six criminal defence solicitors, which is why the phone is answered at four in the morning. Telephone advice before an interview is real advice, wherever you are held. For serious matters we travel. And if we honestly cannot reach you in time, we will say so on the call and help you find someone who can. We would rather lose the case to honesty than win it with a fiction.

> **If the Gardai are waiting**
> Give your name, address and date of birth and nothing more. Say clearly that you want a solicitor before any interview, then ask the member in charge to ring 087 122 3080.

## How it works from custody

1. Ask the member in charge to record that you have requested a solicitor, and name us.
2. We speak to you by phone before any interview, in private.
3. We tell you honestly whether attendance is realistic in your county tonight.
4. Where the matter is serious, we travel; where it cannot wait, we advise by phone and follow up.

## Before you ring

It helps to know the station you or your family member is held at, the time of the arrest, and whether an interview has already started. If you do not know all of it, ring anyway. Your [rights in a Garda station](/your-rights/) are the same in every county, and [advice at the station is free for most people](/legal-aid/).

## Common questions

### Do you cover my county?

We act for clients nationwide. Whether a solicitor can attend in person tonight depends on where you are held and how long the detention is likely to run, and we will tell you that honestly on the phone rather than promise what geography will not allow.

### Is telephone advice enough?

Before an interview it is often the part that matters most, because it is the advice you act on in the room. Where attendance is needed for a serious matter, we travel from Dublin.

### Who answers at night?

Whichever of our six criminal defence solicitors is on call. The out-of-hours number, 087 122 3080, is a rota between six practitioners across five Dublin offices, not one person's mobile. See [nights, weekends and bank holidays](/weekend-out-of-hours/).
