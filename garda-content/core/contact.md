## The urgent number

087 122 3080 is answered outside office hours, at weekends and on bank holidays by whichever of our criminal defence solicitors is on the rota. Use it if:

- you have been arrested and are in a Garda station now
- an interview is about to start
- a family member is in custody and needs a solicitor
- you have been asked to attend a station and have been given a time
- you are due before a court sitting at a weekend or on a bank holiday

When you ring, it helps to have three things ready: the name of the station, the full name and date of birth of the person in custody, and whether any interview has already taken place.

If the line is engaged, a colleague is dealing with another station. Leave a voicemail with a callback number or send a WhatsApp message, and keep your phone with you. There is more on how out of hours cover works on our page about [nights, weekends and bank holidays](/weekend-out-of-hours).

## Common questions

### Which number should I ring?

If somebody is in a Garda station now, or an interview is imminent, ring 087 122 3080 whatever the time. For everything else during office hours, ring (01) 677 9408.

### Can I email instead?

For urgent matters, no. Email is checked during office hours and there may be a delay of hours. Use email for documents, dates and questions about a matter that is already under way.

### Do I need an appointment to call into an office?

Ring first. Criminal defence solicitors spend a good deal of the working day in court, so a phone call means somebody who can actually help you is there when you arrive.

### Can I meet a solicitor at the office nearest to me?

In most cases, yes. Tell us which office suits you when you ring and we will arrange it where we can. Scheduled appointments about court matters are most often held at Ormond Quay.

### What should I have ready when I ring about an arrest?

The name of the Garda station, the full name and date of birth of the person in custody, and anything about their health, age or language that the station needs to know. If you do not have all of it, ring anyway.
