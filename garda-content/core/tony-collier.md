## Background and professional roles

Tony joined Ferrys Solicitors LLP in 2020, having previously run his own criminal practice in Dublin. He is based at the firm's Ormond Quay office.

He is a member of the **Law Society of Ireland Education Faculty**, which means he is involved in the training of solicitors, and a member of the **Criminal Law Committee of the Dublin Solicitors Bar Association**. Between them, those two roles say something specific about the work: this is an area he teaches and contributes to professionally, not only one he practises in.

He writes on criminal justice for the **Law Society Gazette**, on subjects including criminal legal aid, prison remission, extradition and civil restraining orders, and he has commented on criminal justice matters in the national press.

## The courts he appears in

He appears in the **District Court**, the **Circuit Criminal Court**, the **Central Criminal Court** and the **Special Criminal Court**, including in the Criminal Courts of Justice in Dublin.

That spread matters more than it might look on a page. Criminal cases do not stay where they start. A matter that begins as a charge sheet in a District Court on a Monday morning can be sent forward for trial, and the person facing it should not have to change solicitor because the case has outgrown the person representing them. Continuity from the cell to the trial court is the point.

## What the team does

Those are categories of offence. The list below is the other way of cutting it, by stage: the criminal defence work at Ferrys covers the whole life of a case, and it starts long before a courtroom is involved.

- **Garda station attendance and advice**, at any hour, including advice before and during interview.
- **Voluntary attendances**, where a person has been asked to come in and needs to know what that means before agreeing to a time.
- **Bail applications**, at first appearance and in the higher courts.
- **District Court prosecutions**, including road traffic matters, public order, assault, theft and drugs offences.
- **Indictable matters** sent forward for trial in the Circuit Criminal Court and, where the charge requires it, the Central Criminal Court.
- **Matters before the Special Criminal Court.**
- **Appeals** against conviction or sentence.
- **Advice on the consequences that worry people most**: employment, Garda vetting, professional registration and travel.

We do not publish case outcomes or client details, and you should be careful of anyone who does. Every case turns on its own facts, and no solicitor can tell you in advance how yours will end. What we can do is tell you honestly what the process looks like and what your realistic options are within it.

## How we work with you

1. **The first call is with a solicitor.** You will not be taken through a script by somebody who cannot advise you.
2. **We tell you where you actually stand.** That includes the parts you would rather not hear. A comfortable answer at the start is expensive later.
3. **You get the same firm from station to court.** The solicitor who advised you at the interview stage is part of the team that takes the matter on.
4. **We explain each step before it happens,** including what a court date is for and what will and will not be decided on the day.
5. **We are honest about coverage.** Our offices are in Dublin. We act for clients nationwide and will travel for serious matters, and if we cannot reach you in time to be useful, we will say so and help you find someone who can.

## Common questions

### Will Tony Collier personally handle my case?

Ask when you ring and we will tell you plainly who will be dealing with your matter. Criminal defence at Ferrys is done by a team of six, and out of hours attendances are covered on a rota, so the solicitor who attends your station may be a colleague. Every one of them practises in this area.

### Which courts do you appear in?

Tony Collier appears in the District, Circuit Criminal, Central Criminal and Special Criminal Courts, and the firm's criminal work spans those courts. If your matter is sent forward from the District Court, you do not need to instruct a different firm.

### Can you tell me what will happen in my case?

No solicitor can honestly tell you the outcome of a case in advance, and you should be wary of anyone who offers to. What we can do is explain the process, the evidence, the realistic options and the factors a court will consider, so that the decisions you make are informed ones.

### How long has the firm been practising?

Ferrys Solicitors LLP was established in 1989. Criminal defence is a settled part of the firm's work rather than an out of hours sideline.

### I am not in Dublin. Can you still act for me?

We act for clients around the country and will travel for serious matters. For anything urgent, ring 087 122 3080 and we will tell you honestly whether we can attend in time, and help you find someone local if we cannot.
