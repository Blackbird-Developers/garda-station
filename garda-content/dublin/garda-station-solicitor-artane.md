Our Artane office stands at the corner where the Malahide Road meets Kilmore Road, in the middle of the part of the northside it serves. If you have been arrested anywhere in Dublin 5 or the districts around it, this is the office we work from and the stations below are the ones we know best.

## The stations this office covers

Coolock is the busiest station in this part of the city and the one we attend most often from Artane. We also cover Raheny, Clontarf, Santry and Ballymun, and we travel out to Howth, Malahide and Swords when an arrest happens further north or along the coast.

The communities those stations serve are the ones on our doorstep: Artane, Donnycarney, Beaumont, Killester, Harmonstown, Edenmore, Kilbarrack, Marino, Fairview and Clontarf. If you are a parent ringing about a teenager, tell us the station name and we will take it from there.

> **If the Gardai are waiting**
> A voluntary attendance is still an interview, and what is said in it is evidence. Get advice before you go in, not after. Ring 087 122 3080.

## Getting here

The office is on the Malahide Road at the Kilmore Road junction, close to Artane Castle shopping centre. The Malahide Road is one of the main routes between the northside suburbs and the city centre and carries regular bus services in both directions. Coolock, Beaumont and Donnycarney are all within a short local journey.

## Northside arrests and the courts

Most criminal cases arising from arrests on this side of the city are dealt with at the Criminal Courts of Justice on Parkgate Street, and some District Court business for the north of the county is heard at Swords. The solicitor who attends the station is part of the same team that handles the court dates, so you are not starting again with a stranger.

## If it is the middle of the night

The 24 hour number reaches the criminal defence solicitor on the rota, not this office alone. Whichever station you are in, and whatever the hour, that is the number to ring.

1. Ring 087 122 3080 and name the station you are being held in.
2. Say whether you have been arrested or asked to attend voluntarily.
3. Ask the member in charge to note that you have requested a solicitor.
4. Do not answer questions about the allegation until you have taken advice.
5. If you are ringing on behalf of a young person, say their age, because different arrangements apply to children in custody.

## Common questions

### My child has been taken to Coolock station. What happens now?

A child in custody has particular protections and a parent or guardian is normally expected to be present. Ring 087 122 3080 straight away and we will explain what should be happening.

### Do you attend Swords and Malahide as well?

Yes, we travel out from Artane for stations further north. Ring and we will tell you honestly how long it will take us to get there.

### I have been asked to come into Raheny station next week. Should I bring a solicitor?

Speak to us first. In many cases it makes sense for the solicitor to arrange the appointment and attend with you.

### Does using the Artane office cost more than the city centre one?

No. Which office you deal with makes no difference to how your case is handled or charged. Ask us about costs and the State scheme for people in custody when you ring.
