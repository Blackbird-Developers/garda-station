Our Rialto office is on the South Circular Road, between Dolphin's Barn and Kilmainham, and it is the office we work from for the south inner city. If you have been arrested anywhere in Dublin 8 or the streets around it, a solicitor from this office is usually only minutes away from the station.

**443 South Circular Road, Rialto, Dublin 8, D08 F6X9**
**Direct line: (01) 454 4275**
**24 hours: 087 122 3080**

## The stations this office covers

Kilmainham is the nearest station to us and the one we attend most often from Rialto. We also cover Kevin Street, Sundrive Road, Crumlin, Rathmines and Terenure, and we go into Pearse Street and Donnybrook where the arrest happened further east.

The neighbourhoods those stations serve are the ones around this office: Rialto itself, Dolphin's Barn, Inchicore, Islandbridge, Kilmainham, The Liberties, Portobello, Harold's Cross, Drimnagh and Crumlin. It is a densely built part of the city and the station you end up in is not always the one nearest your home, so tell us the station name rather than the address when you ring.

> **If the Gardai are waiting**
> Do not try to explain or clear things up before you get advice. Say you want a solicitor, ask the member in charge to phone us, and wait. Ring 087 122 3080.

## Getting here

The South Circular Road runs east to west across the south inner city, and number 443 sits on it in Rialto. The Luas red line has a stop at Rialto, and the road is on bus routes running between the city centre and the south west of the city. St James's Hospital and the Coombe are both nearby, which is worth knowing if a family member has been taken to hospital before or after an arrest.

## Ringing at night

The 24 hour number is not tied to this office. It goes to whichever criminal defence solicitor is on the rota, and that solicitor will attend whatever station you are in. Nobody has to sit in a cell until Monday morning waiting for a particular door to open.

1. Ring 087 122 3080 as soon as you can, even if you think you will be released shortly.
2. Give the station name, your name and your date of birth.
3. Tell us if you have been asked to sign anything already.
4. Ask for the interview to be paused until you have spoken to a solicitor.
5. Keep a note of the time of arrest and the names of the members involved if you can.

## Common questions

### I was lifted in the Liberties. Which station am I likely to be in?

It depends on where exactly the arrest happened, and Kevin Street and Kilmainham both cover parts of that area. Ring us and we will find out where you are being held.

### Can you deal with the case afterwards, or just the station?

Both. The solicitor who attends the station stays with the case through the District Court and beyond if it goes further.

### My son is in Kilmainham and I cannot get any information. Can you?

We can contact the member in charge on his behalf and confirm that he is in custody and that a solicitor has been requested. Ring 087 122 3080 with his details.

### Do I have to come to the Rialto office to become a client?

No. Most people first speak to us by phone, often from a station. Arrangements about where and when to meet can be made afterwards.
