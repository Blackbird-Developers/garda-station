## The stations this office covers

Ballyfermot station is the closest to us and the one we attend most often. From here we also cover Clondalkin, Ronanstown, Lucan and Kilmainham, and we go on to Blanchardstown and Tallaght where an arrest takes someone further out.

Between them, those stations take in Ballyfermot, Cherry Orchard, Bluebell, Inchicore, Chapelizod, Palmerstown, Clondalkin, Neilstown, Quarryvale and Lucan. West Dublin is spread out, so if you are unsure which station a family member has been taken to, ring us and we will find out rather than sending you around them one by one.

> **If the Gardai are waiting**
> You are entitled to speak to a solicitor before you are interviewed, and asking for one is not an admission of anything. Say it plainly and ask the member in charge to phone us. Ring 087 122 3080.

## Getting here

The office is on Ballyfermot Road, the main road running the length of the area, close to the civic centre and library. Regular buses run along it between Ballyfermot and the city centre, and the road connects east to Chapelizod and Inchicore and west towards Cherry Orchard and the Naas Road. From the Criminal Courts of Justice at Parkgate Street it is a short run out along the Chapelizod bypass.

## What we deal with from this office

Most of the work here starts with a phone call from a station: a public order incident, an assault allegation, a road traffic matter, a search of a house, a theft or a drugs allegation. The concerns people raise first are rarely about court. They are about a job, a Garda vetting form, a visa, or what to tell an employer. Raise those on the first call, because they can shape how a case is approached.

1. Ring 087 122 3080 and tell us which station you are in.
2. Say whether a search has taken place and whether anything was seized.
3. Ask the member in charge to record your request for a solicitor.
4. Do not discuss the allegation with anyone, including others in custody.
5. Write down the time of arrest and keep any paperwork you are given.

## Cover outside office hours

The 24 hour number does not ring in this building. It reaches the criminal defence solicitor on the rota, so a Saturday night arrest in Clondalkin gets the same response as a Tuesday morning one in Ballyfermot.

## Common questions

### Which station covers Cherry Orchard?

Arrests in that area are usually processed at Ballyfermot or Ronanstown depending on where exactly the incident happened. Ring us and we will confirm where the person is.

### Gardai are at my door with a search warrant. What do I do?

Do not obstruct them, ask for a copy of the warrant, and ring 087 122 3080. Say as little as possible about anything found until you have advice.

### Will the same solicitor stay with my case?

Yes, wherever possible. The solicitor who attends the station is part of the criminal defence team that will take the case forward.

### Can I use this office if I live in Lucan or Clondalkin?

Yes. Any of our five Dublin offices can act for you, and the choice makes no difference to how the case is run.
