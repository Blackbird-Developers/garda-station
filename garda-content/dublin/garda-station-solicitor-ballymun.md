## The stations this office covers

Ballymun and Santry are the two nearest stations and the ones we attend most often from here. We also cover Finglas, Cabra, Mountjoy and Coolock, and we go out to Dublin Airport, Swords and Blanchardstown when an arrest happens further north or west.

The areas those stations serve include Ballymun, Poppintree, Santry, Whitehall, Beaumont, Glasnevin, Finglas, Charlestown, Meakstown and the industrial estates and hotels around the airport road. This office also picks up a particular kind of call: people detained at or near Dublin Airport, often travelling, often with very little time.

> **If the Gardai are waiting**
> Ask for a solicitor before any interview, and say nothing about the allegation in the meantime, even in casual conversation. Ring 087 122 3080.

## Arrests near the airport

If you have been stopped or arrested at the airport, tell us that on the first call and tell us about any flight. It does not change your rights, but it changes what needs to happen and in what order. Do not assume a matter will be dealt with quickly so that you can travel on, and do not answer questions in the hope that it will speed things up.

Bus services run along the Ballymun Road corridor between the city centre and Ballymun and Santry, and the office is close to the Ballymun town centre area.

## Ringing the 24 hour number

The number is answered by the criminal defence solicitor on the rota, wherever that solicitor is based. It is not a diverted office line and it is not one person's mobile, so a call at four in the morning from Finglas gets a real answer.

1. Ring 087 122 3080 and give the station name and the time of arrest.
2. Say whether an interview has already started.
3. Ask the member in charge to record that you have asked for a solicitor.
4. Do not sign a memo of interview or a statement before taking advice.
5. If you have a flight, court date or work commitment, mention it on the first call.

## Common questions

### How long can I be held before I have to be released or charged?

That depends on the legislation you are being detained under, and in some cases a period of detention can be extended. Ring us and we will explain what applies in your situation.

### Do I have to answer the Gardai's questions?

You have a right to silence, but in certain limited circumstances a court may be permitted to draw inferences from a failure to answer. That is exactly why the decision needs legal advice before the interview, not after.

### Someone has been arrested at Dublin Airport. Which station will they be in?

They may be dealt with at the station at the airport or taken to another station nearby. Ring 087 122 3080 with their name and we will locate them.

### Is there a charge for the phone call?

No. Ringing us to find out where you stand costs nothing, and we will tell you plainly what happens next before anything is agreed.
