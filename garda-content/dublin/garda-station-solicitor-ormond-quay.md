Our Ormond Quay office sits on the north quays, a short distance downriver from the Four Courts and a straight run west along the quays to the Criminal Courts of Justice at Parkgate Street. If you have been arrested in the city centre, this is usually the office closest to the station you are being held in.

**Inn Chambers, 15 Ormond Quay Upper, Dublin 7, D07 YK6A**
**Direct line: (01) 677 9408**
**24 hours: 087 122 3080**

## The stations this office covers

Ormond Quay is the office nearest the Bridewell, which is minutes away on Chancery Street and where a great many city centre arrests are processed. From here we also attend Store Street, Fitzgibbon Street, Mountjoy and Cabra in the north inner city, and Pearse Street and Kevin Street on the south side of the river.

Because the office is central, it also serves people who live outside the city but are arrested in it: a night out that ended badly, an incident on the quays or in Temple Bar, a road traffic matter on the way through town.

> **If the Gardai are waiting**
> Say clearly that you want a solicitor before any interview and ask the member in charge to phone us. Give your name, address and date of birth, and nothing further about the allegation until we have spoken. Ring 087 122 3080.

## Getting here

Ormond Quay Upper runs along the north bank of the Liffey between the Ha'penny Bridge and the Four Courts, close to Capel Street and the Jervis area. The quays are served by a large number of city bus routes, and the Luas red line runs nearby along the north quays with stops at Jervis and Four Courts. If you are coming from the Criminal Courts of Justice, the office is a short journey east along the river.

## If you are ringing out of hours

The 24 hour number reaches whichever of our criminal defence solicitors is on the rota that night. You do not need to wait for this office, or any office, to open. Six solicitors across five Dublin offices means the phone is answered by somebody who can actually move, not by a message service.

1. Ring 087 122 3080 and say which station you are in and roughly when you were arrested.
2. Tell us whether an interview has started or is about to start.
3. Ask the member in charge to record your request for a solicitor.
4. Say nothing about the allegation, and sign nothing, until we have spoken.
5. If you are ringing for someone else, have their full name and date of birth ready.

## Common questions

### Someone I know is in the Bridewell. What do I do?

Ring 087 122 3080 with their name and, if you have it, their date of birth and the time they were taken in. We will contact the station directly and tell you what we can find out.

### Is this the office nearest the Criminal Courts of Justice?

Yes. Ormond Quay is the closest of our five offices to Parkgate Street, which is where most Dublin criminal court business is heard.

### Can I call in to the office instead of phoning?

If your matter is not urgent, ring (01) 677 9408 first and we will arrange a time. If you are in custody or due to be interviewed, use the 24 hour number instead.

### I was arrested in town but I live outside Dublin. Does that matter?

Not for the station stage. The case will usually be dealt with where the alleged offence happened, so a city centre arrest normally means a Dublin court.
