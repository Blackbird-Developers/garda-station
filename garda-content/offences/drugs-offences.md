Most people arrested in a drugs case have never been in a Garda station before, and most of them talk too much in the first ten minutes. Whether you were stopped on a street, searched in a car, arrested at a house or taken from a festival campsite, the order is the same: solicitor first, questions later.

Drugs cases run from simple possession of a small amount for personal use to possession for the purpose of sale or supply, which is treated far more seriously and does not require anyone to have seen a sale. What usually decides which of those a case becomes is the circumstances: how the drugs were packaged, what else was found, what was on a phone, and what was said in interview.

> **If the Gardai are waiting**
> Do not explain whose it is or who you were with. Ask for a solicitor and answer no questions until you have spoken to one. Ring 087 122 3080.

## How these cases usually begin

- **On the street or in a car.** A search on foot of a suspicion, often at night.
- **At a house.** A search warrant executed early in the morning, with everyone present detained and questioned.
- **At festivals and large events.** Searches at gates and inside campsites, sometimes assisted by detection dogs. People are often arrested far from home, at night, without a charged phone or a friend nearby.
- **Through someone else's phone.** Messages recovered in another investigation lead to a knock weeks later.

One point worth knowing: buying for a group of friends and holding it for the night is not automatically treated as personal possession, and nor is minding something for someone else.

## What the Garda station stage looks like

1. **Search and seizure.** What is taken is recorded. Cash, scales, bags and phones are commonly seized alongside any suspected controlled drug.
2. **Detention and questioning** under whichever power applies. The period you may be held depends on the legislation used and can sometimes be extended.
3. **Your phone.** Expect it to be examined. Interview questions are often built around messages, contacts and payment apps.
4. **Analysis takes time.** Suspected drugs go for scientific analysis and a certificate follows later. This is why cases go quiet for months.
5. **Release, then a decision.** Many people are released without charge on the night and receive a summons afterwards, once analysis is complete and the Director of Public Prosecutions has directed how the matter should proceed.

## What happens afterwards

Simple possession is generally dealt with in the District Court. Sale or supply allegations may stay there or be sent forward to the Circuit Criminal Court, depending on the direction given by the Director of Public Prosecutions. In Dublin, District Court criminal business is heard at the Criminal Courts of Justice on Parkgate Street. There may also be a separate application about seized cash or a vehicle.

## What people worry about

Travel is the anxiety that surprises people most. A drugs conviction can affect visa applications and entry to countries including the United States, Canada and Australia, and it can affect working holiday and study permissions. If you are here on a permission, take advice early.

Then there is employment and vetting. Garda vetting matters for anyone working with children or vulnerable adults, in healthcare, in security or in many public sector roles. Students face college disciplinary processes, and professional bodies in nursing, teaching, medicine and financial services have their own reporting expectations. Court lists are public and District Court cases are reported locally, so raise any concern about your name early.

## Common questions

### It was for my own use. Why are they talking about supply?

Because the assessment is made on all the circumstances, including quantity, packaging and phone material. That is why advice before interview matters.

### I was released and heard nothing. Is it over?

Not necessarily. Analysis and directions take time, and a summons can arrive months later. Keep your address up to date.

### Do I have to give the Gardai my phone passcode?

There are circumstances in which a demand can be made, and consequences attach to some refusals. Ask for your solicitor before deciding.

### Will this show up on Garda vetting?

Vetting disclosure depends on the outcome and the type of matter. Ask us before you complete any vetting form or make a disclosure to an employer.
