A rota, not one mobile phone

Plenty of firms advertise a 24-hour number. The question worth asking is what happens when it rings at three in the morning on a bank holiday, and whether the person who answers can actually attend.

Criminal defence at Ferrys is a team of six solicitors working across five Dublin offices. That is what makes genuine out-of-hours cover possible: when one solicitor is already in a station or in court, another is available. You are not waiting on one individual's availability.

What you get when you call out of hours

  • A criminal defence solicitor — not an answering service taking a message.
  • Immediate advice on the phone, before any interview begins.
  • Attendance at the station where that is what the situation needs.
  • Eligibility for the Garda Station Legal Advice Scheme assessed there and then.

Weekend court

If someone is charged and held over a weekend or a public holiday, they must be brought before a sitting of the District Court. We deal with weekend and holiday court sittings, including bail applications, so that nobody spends longer in custody than necessary.

Bank holidays and Christmas

Cover runs through public holidays and over Christmas. These are periods when arrests rise and availability elsewhere falls.

What to do if you cannot reach someone immediately

If for any reason you do not get straight through, send a WhatsApp message or a text with your name, the station you are at or heading to, and a number to reach you. We will come back to you. In the meantime: say nothing about the allegation until you have spoken to a solicitor.
