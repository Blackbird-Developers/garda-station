Tony Collier

Partner, Criminal Defence — Ferrys Solicitors LLP

Tony Collier is one of Ireland's most experienced criminal defence solicitors. He joined Ferrys Solicitors LLP in 2020, bringing with him an established Dublin criminal practice, and he has been involved in defending some of the most significant criminal cases in the history of the State.

His work has contributed to the protection of the rights of people detained, charged and tried for criminal offences — which is the reason a service like this one exists.

He appears in the District Court, the Circuit Criminal Court, the Central Criminal Court and the Special Criminal Court.

  • Member, Law Society of Ireland — Education Faculty
  • Member, Dublin Solicitors Bar Association — Criminal Law Committee
  • Partner, Ferrys Solicitors LLP, Ormond Quay
  • Regular media commentator on criminal justice and criminal legal aid

He does not just practise it — he teaches it

Tony's membership of the Law Society of Ireland's Education Faculty means he is involved in the training of other solicitors, and his membership of the DSBA's Criminal Law Committee places him within the professional body that represents criminal practitioners in Dublin.

He is also a working voice on criminal justice policy. He has spoken publicly on the criminal legal aid dispute and its consequences for access to representation — the practical question of whether people facing criminal charges can get a solicitor at all.

Why this matters when you are choosing

Garda station work rewards experience of the specific process: what can be asked, what can be inferred, when to intervene, and how a decision made at 4am plays out in a courtroom two years later. That is judgement built up over a very large number of these situations.
