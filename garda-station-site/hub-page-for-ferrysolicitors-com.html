Garda Station Solicitor — Dublin & Nationwide, 24 Hours

If you have been arrested, or asked to attend a Garda station to give a statement, you are entitled to legal advice before you are interviewed. Our criminal defence team attends Garda stations across Dublin at any hour.

If the Gardai are waiting

Tell the member in charge you want your own solicitor before any interview, and call 087 122 3080. Say nothing about the allegation until we have spoken.

What we do

We advise you before questioning begins, attend with you at the station, and make sure the process is fair — then we deal with whatever follows, from station bail through to trial in the District, Circuit, Central or Special Criminal Court.

Why the team matters

A 24-hour number is only as good as the person who answers it. Criminal defence at Ferrys is a team of six solicitors across five Dublin offices, led by Tony Collier — a member of the Law Society of Ireland's Education Faculty and of the DSBA's Criminal Law Committee. Round-the-clock cover is a rota, not one mobile phone.
