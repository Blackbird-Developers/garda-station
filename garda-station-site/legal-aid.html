The Garda Station Legal Advice Scheme

Ireland operates a scheme under which a person detained in a Garda station can receive legal advice, and have a solicitor attend, without paying for it themselves. It is administered by the Legal Aid Board and it is means tested.

The practical point is simple: ask for a solicitor first and sort the paperwork afterwards. Do not decline legal advice because you are worried about a bill.

We will tell you where you stand on the phone

When you call, we will take some brief details and tell you whether you are likely to qualify — before you commit to anything. If you do not qualify, we will tell you clearly what our fees would be. No one should be surprised by a bill.

Criminal legal aid in court

Legal aid at the Garda station and legal aid for court proceedings are separate things. If you are charged, the court can grant criminal legal aid where your means are insufficient and the case is serious enough that legal representation is essential in the interests of justice.

The court assesses this. In general terms it considers:

  • Means — your income, assets, dependants and outgoings.
  • Gravity — the seriousness of the charge and what is at stake for you.
  • Exceptional circumstances — which can be relevant even where means alone might not qualify you.

Where a case is sent forward to the Circuit Criminal Court or Central Criminal Court, a legal aid certificate granted earlier will generally carry through.

You still choose your own solicitor

Being granted legal aid does not mean a solicitor is assigned to you and you must accept them. You are entitled to nominate the solicitor you want, and to change solicitor if you wish. Legal aid does not take that choice away.

If you do not qualify

We will give you a clear picture of costs before you engage us, in writing, and we will tell you what is likely to be involved at each stage rather than presenting it all at the end. If your circumstances change during a case, tell us — eligibility can be revisited.

Do not let cost delay the call

Detention clocks run. The advice is most valuable before an interview, not after. Call 087 122 3080 and we will deal with eligibility as part of that call.

Talk to a solicitor before you talk to the Gardai.

One call, at any hour. If you qualify for legal aid it costs you nothing — and either way you will know exactly where you stand.
